We tested it · 2026-11-17

The app was installed on our Shopify development store, which we set up, and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. On 17 November 2026 the session tested StoreFAQ on a Shopify development store on the Free plan: the install, three test FAQs, the FAQ schema switch, the preview of the app page /a/faqs, the plan cards and the Professional approval screen (not approved). The preview said that no FAQs were found until a group was made, because FAQs without a group do not show; after the group Shipping and returns was made the question and its answer showed in the preview, about 4 minutes after the install. The structured data was not seen, nothing was paid, and the app was uninstalled afterwards. Install: the install form on the App Store page was opened in our own tab at about 17:40 and the permissions of the app were approved at about 17:41; the app opened on the page Choose the perfect plan for your store. The admin was in English. The version of the app is 3.7.7 by Storeware. Plan: the Free plan ($0) was chosen at once, with no card, no phone number and no account on the vendor site. Core action, an FAQ page: at about 17:42 the first FAQ, How long does shipping take? Was made in the group Ungrouped and the message FAQ created appeared. In the setup guide Enable FAQ Schema was clicked, which gave the message FAQ Schema toggled. And the page Settings then shows Enable FAQ Schema with the label On. A second FAQ, What is your return policy? Was also made as Ungrouped. Result: the preview of the app page /a/faqs in the theme editor said Sorry, No FAQs found. The app gives the reason: if you keep the FAQ group as Ungrouped it will not show on the front end. FAQs without a group are therefore invisible. A group Shipping and returns was made (the first try hung on a spinner, the second worked) and a third FAQ, Do you ship internationally? Was put in it. At about 17:44 the preview showed the group and, opened, the answer Yes, we ship to most countries. Shipping costs show at checkout. So the first visible result took about 4 minutes and about 15 steps from the install, and only after the group was made; the app does not warn about the missing group when an FAQ is saved. The address of the FAQ page is an app proxy path, /a/faqs, on the store domain. FAQ schema: only the On switch was seen. The structured data (JSON-LD) in the output of the page was NOT read, because the preview is a cross-origin frame and the store has a password. Whether the FAQ schema is really output, and whether it is valid, was not tested. Plans: the plan cards in the app (Black Friday offer, code BFCMSALE40, 40 percent off) show Professional $7.99 struck through to $4.79 a month, Growth $14.99 to $8.99 and Enterprise $49.99 to $29.99. Free: Up to 100 FAQ Page Views and AI Conversations 25 (Lifetime). Professional: Monthly plan includes 500 page views, extra usage billed at $0.0075/view. Growth: 2,000 page views, extra usage billed at $0.0075/view. Both: $0.30 per extra conversation after limit. Enterprise: Unlimited usage of page views. Billing for page views and conversations is capped at $7.99, $14.99 and $49.99 a month individually. Differences with the dossier: the dossier gives an overage of $0.0159 a view on Professional and $0.0075 on Growth; the app shows $0.0075 a view on Professional as well, so the Professional overage in the app is lower than in the dossier. The Black Friday offer BFCMSALE40 is not in the dossier. Checkout: Subscribe now on Professional opened a dialog with Subtotal $7.99, Discount: (BFCMSALE40) -$3.2 and Total $4.79, and then the Shopify screen Approve subscription: plan professional-monthly, $7,99 struck through and $4,79 USD every 30 days, an App spending limit of a maximum of $15,98 USD per 30 days (twice the $7.99 cap), a badge 40% off 1 charge, a row Add payment method and a greyed out Approve button, with Total $4,79 due 17 Nov plus any applicable taxes. The sentence about a test charge was NOT on the screen and a payment method had to be added, so nothing was approved; no card was entered. Cancelling: no paid plan was started, so nothing could be cancelled; the way to stop is to remove the app in Settings, Apps, which was done after the test. Not tested: the two Ungrouped FAQs (they stay invisible until they get a group), the AI chatbot and Instant Answer, Write with AI (it asks for an OpenAI key), Live Chat, import and export of FAQs as CSV, multilingual FAQs on the Professional plan, what happens when the limit of 100 page views is passed, and the JSON-LD of the FAQ schema. What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

You install it to get SEO-friendly FAQ pages that rank and get read; every read is a billable unit. The free plan stops at 100 views and Professional at 500, which a product-page FAQ on a store with any traffic burns through in days, and then it is $0.0159 a view, up to $7.99 a month in extra charges by the cap storefaq.io publishes; neither page says what happens to your FAQ pages once that cap is reached.

Unlimited views arrive only on the $49.99 tier. Success on the cheap plans costs you money, which is an odd shape for a pricing page and the first thing to model before you install. The AI allowance is the second meter and it is tight. Twenty-five conversations for the entire life of a free account, fifty a month at $7.99, a hundred at $14.99, and $0.30 each after that. Two hundred extra conversations in a month is $60, more than the top tier costs outright, so if the chatbot is the reason you are here, price the conversations rather than the subscription and jump straight to the tier that fits.

And know the boundary of the tool. This builds FAQ content, schema and a light chat layer. It is not a helpdesk: no ticketing, no agent queues, no multichannel reporting. Anything a shopper asks that an FAQ cannot answer still lands in whatever inbox you had before.

Professional is the right plan for 440 views

The meter here is FAQ page views, which is the thing this app exists to increase, so success costs money, and the tiers are worth doing properly (read 2026-08-21 on the Shopify listing). Free covers 100 views. Professional is $7.99 for 500 views with overage at $0.0159 a view. Growth is $14.99 for 2,000 views with overage at $0.0075. storefaq.io caps those overage charges at $7.99 a month on Professional and $14.99 on Growth (read 17 November 2026).

Enterprise is $49.99 a month, or $479.90 a year for a 20% saving, with unlimited views. Put the overage into the comparison and the middle of the ladder collapses. Professional plus overage reaches $14.99, Growth's price, at just 940 views a month. So Professional is only ever the cheapest plan between 500 and 940 views: a window 440 views wide. Any FAQ page with genuine traffic passes through it in days, and after that you are paying Growth's price for Professional's allowance.

Growth holds up much longer. It stays cheapest until about 6,667 views a month, which is where its overage has added $35 and brought it level with Enterprise's unlimited $49.99. Above that, Enterprise is simply the answer, and 6,667 FAQ page views a month is a modest store, so a shop with real traffic belongs on the $49.99 plan, which is 6.3 times the entry price. That is the honest reading of this pricing page: it is cheap for a store nobody visits.

The AI conversation meter runs the opposite way to the view meter and deserves separate treatment. Conversations cost $0.30 each above your allowance on every tier, there is no volume break at all, while the included rate is $0.16 on Professional, $0.15 on Growth and $0.10 on Enterprise. Overage is therefore two to three times the included price, so on conversations you should always upgrade rather than overrun. Two hundred extra conversations is $60, more than Enterprise costs outright.

And note the free tier's allowance is 25 conversations for the lifetime of the account, not per month: it is a sample, not a plan. Finally, know the boundary. What you are buying is FAQ content, the schema that goes with it and a light chat layer on top. Ticketing, agent queues and multichannel reporting are not part of it, so a question the FAQ cannot answer still ends up in the inbox you were already using.

    How to actually use StoreFAQ

    Estimate your FAQ page views before you choose, because that is the meter. Professional charges $0.0159 for each view past 500, so above about 940 views a month it already costs more than Growth at $14.99. Both overage lines are capped, though: storefaq.io says Professional's page view charges stop at $7.99 a month and Growth's at $14.99, and the plan cards in the app say the same of conversation charges (read 17 November 2026).

    On those caps Professional never costs more than $15.98 a month for page views and Growth never more than $29.98, so on page views alone Enterprise at $49.99 is never the cheaper plan; its case is the 500 AI conversations a month and priority support. If the caps work as written, a busy store pays less on capped Professional than on Growth past about 2,130 views a month. That is our arithmetic on the published caps, and neither page says whether FAQ pages keep showing once a cap is reached, so ask StoreFAQ before you plan around it.
