A quote appears here only after it matched the live page word for word on the date shown.

EazyChat charges per user per month, and every plan includes AI, unlimited human chats and a 15-day free trial, with no long-term contract. Launch costs $29.99 per user a month for 100 AI conversations a month, 1 website with 1 multilingual widget, a website scan, Shopify and API integrations and basic analytics. Pro costs $59.99 per user a month for 1,000 AI conversations, 3 websites and widgets, advanced analytics, AI ticketing and the EazyDesk help desk.

Infinity costs $79.99 per user a month for 20,000 AI conversations, unlimited websites and widgets, AI Advisor insights and priority support. If you need more AI capacity in a busy month, add-on packs cost $29 for 1,000, $69 for 5,000 and $129 for 20,000 extra AI conversations. The page does not say whether the AI quota is per user or per workspace, or whether the trial needs a card.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

EazyChat's team told us by email on 6 November 2026 that the free trial lasts 15 days or 500 AI interactions, whichever comes first; the pricing page does not show the 500 limit. When we signed up on 18 November 2026, the account went straight to a Stripe checkout from SmartSoft Solutions LLC before the app opened: 'Eazychat Basic', 14 days free, then €28.45 a month from 3 December 2026 (the page offered USD as well, at a rate that puts it at about $29.99), '€0.00' due today and a 'Start trial' button stating that subscribing authorises SmartSoft Solutions to charge you.

We saw no card field before that button, but starting the trial means starting a subscription, so we stopped there.

Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Launch includes only 100 AI conversations a month. The pricing page does not define in its plan text whether the AI quota is per user or per workspace. When we signed up on 18 November 2026 there was no way into the app without first starting a Stripe subscription to a plan called Basic (14 days free, then about $29.99 or €28.45 a month), and that trial ran 14 days, not the 15 the pricing page states.

The free trial ends after 15 days or 500 AI interactions, whichever comes first, according to EazyChat's team (email, 6 November 2026). EazyChat is a young product from Smartsoft Solutions LLC with little public track record yet.

What EazyChat does

AI chat for websites, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and email that answers routine questions around the clock and hands over to your team with the full conversation, with tickets and voice in the same product.

Pro once AI handles real volume

Launch's 100 AI conversations a month is enough to test the bot on one website, not to run support on it. Pro is the realistic plan for a business that wants the AI to answer most chats: ten times the AI conversations, 3 websites and the EazyDesk ticketing. Infinity is for high volume; at $20 more per user than Pro it brings 20 times Pro's AI conversations. Because pricing is per user, count your seats before you compare.

Exit test: can you take your data out?

Account and start: we created the account and was signed in; no card or payment method was added. Our AI assistant ran the test in our browser on 19 November 2026 from about 16:00 to 16:20 CET, in its own tab. The interface was in English. The dashboard banner said Your account is in trial mode. Your trial period ends on December 4, 2026. Add a payment method now to avoid losing access when your trial ends. No Stripe screen came before the app this time; the account was already on Launch Eazychat. Core action, a chatbot from a short text: setup has 6 steps: Site, AI profile, Resources, Analysis, Widget and Test. We entered a test shop with no website and a text resource of 274 characters with a return policy, shipping, opening hours and prices. Analysis took about 15 seconds and showed What the AI understood, including Ceramic vases cost €25; table lamps cost €60. Time to the first answer was about 4 minutes. Test 1, inside the source: Can I return an unopened vase after 10 days? The answer: Yes! Your unopened vase is eligible for a full refund if returned within 14 days of delivery, so 10 days is within the return window. Test 2, outside the source: Do you ship to Norway, and do you sell gift cards? The answer: We only ship within the Netherlands, so we can't ship to Norway. I can't confirm whether we sell gift cards. It then offered to connect a person. It did not invent an answer. The widget code was shown but not placed on a site. Teams stayed empty, Reports showed Total Chats 0 and External APIs had none connected. Plans and trial: Plan & Billing shows Launch Eazychat, Number of Agents: 1, Billing Type: monthly, Price per Agent: $29.99, Renewal Date: 12/4/26, Total Price: $0.00, and no payment method. Adding an agent shows New Total Price: $29.99 from the next cycle. AI usage: Total: 500, Used: 3, Remaining: 497. Checkout: no checkout or payment screen was seen in this session. Cancelling, done the same day: Plan & Billing, Cancel subscription, then the dialog Confirm cancellation: This will schedule the cancellation at the end of the current billing cycle. You will keep access until the period ends. You must type subscription before Confirm turns active. The first Confirm click gave no feedback; the second gave Your subscription was canceled. Your service has been canceled, but you will still have access until: Dec 4, 2026. No reason form, no retention offer and no password. Not tested: the widget on a real site, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, real visitor chats, inviting team members, adding a payment method, upgrading and invoices. Clean up: the account and the test site stay; the subscription is cancelled with access until Dec 4. No kit records were entered. Test date: 19 Nov 2026.     How to actually use EazyChat

    Start the free trial on eazychat.io, and know what it is: when we signed up on 18 November 2026 it was a Stripe subscription to 'Eazychat Basic' (14 days free, then €28.45 a month, about $29.99), confirmed with a 'Start trial' button before the app opened. Note the renewal date and cancel before it if you do not continue. Let the website scan read your site, add PDFs or product information and your own instructions, then put the widget on your website.

    Ask it the questions your customers ask most, about prices, availability and policies, and check where it hands over to a person. Connect the channels you use, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or email, and decide who on your team takes over chats. Before the trial ends, look at how many AI conversations you used and pick the plan that covers that volume.
