We tested it · 2026-11-18

We created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. On 18 November 2026 the session linked five help articles of a made up snowboard store to the Fin AI Agent of Intercom under the free 14 day Advanced trial and tested Fin with ten questions in the Batch Test. Fin answered 9 correctly and 1 wrongly, each in about 10 seconds: it said the store does not carry helmets, although none of the articles says so, and it gave Content (4) as its source. Fin never names the article in its answer; only the evaluation panel shows the number of sources. A new article has to be published to the help center and then to Fin before Fin can use it. The prices are unchanged against the dossier (annual Essential $19 for new customers, Advanced $85, Expert $132; monthly $29, $99 and $139; Fin $0.99 per outcome), and the monthly new-customer price of $29 with 25% off is not in the dossier. The payment screen was reached and nothing was entered, and tax was not shown.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Advanced and Expert cost $85 and $132 a seat. The cheaper Essential price is shown as a new-customer offer. We have not paid for a plan, so real-world Fin accuracy is unknown to us.

What Intercom does

Customer service platform that combines a helpdesk and shared inbox with the Fin AI agent, priced per seat plus a fee per resolved conversation.

Advanced adds automation; Expert adds SSO, HIPAA and SLAs

The three plans share the Fin AI Agent, the messenger and the shared inbox. Advanced at $85 a seat adds workflow automation, multiple inboxes and 20 free Lite seats, and Expert at $132 adds SSO, HIPAA support, SLAs and 50 free Lite seats, per the pricing page. Most small teams start on Essential and move up only when they need automation or compliance features. Remember that Fin is billed on top of the seat price.

What upgrading does not change is the Fin bill: every plan pays the same $0.99 per outcome. The seat prices above are the annual rate; billed monthly they are $39, $99 and $139 (help article read 17 November 2026).

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Intercom?

    Exit check 2026-11-18; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does Intercom cost?

    Per the pricing page and Intercom's help article 'Fin and Intercom plans explained', both read on 17 November 2026, in US dollars per seat per month: Essential $29, Advanced $85 and Expert $132 when billed annually, and $39, $99 and $139 when billed monthly. New customers see Essential at $19 on the annual view. Every plan includes the Fin AI Agent, which is charged on top at $0.99 per outcome.

    What is a Fin outcome?

    Intercom bills Fin per outcome. The vendor help article says an outcome counts when the customer confirms the answer was satisfactory or leaves without asking for more help. The pricing page says you never pay if Fin cannot answer or fails to resolve the issue.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes, 14 days with no credit card required, per the pricing FAQ and the help article read on 17 November 2026. The trial includes your chosen plan, Fin and the Pro, Proactive Support Plus and Copilot add-ons; after it you confirm a plan and add payment details. Note that the add-ons are paid extras once the trial ends.

    Can I use Fin without the Intercom helpdesk?

    Yes. Fin is sold standalone for an existing helpdesk such as Salesforce, from $0.99 per outcome with no seats. The page notes a minimum monthly commitment, for example 50 outcomes.

    Are there extra seats?

    Advanced includes 20 free Lite seats and Expert includes 50, per the pricing page. The Essential plan lists none.

    What do the add-ons cost?

    The pricing page lists add-ons such as Copilot, Proactive Support Plus and Pro, with starting prices from $29 per agent a month to $100 a month. They are priced separately from the plans.

    Is there a discount for startups?

    The Early Stage programme offers startups under $10M raised with 15 employees or fewer 96% off. You apply on the pricing page, and we have not tested the approval.

    What do we not know?

    What a real monthly bill looks like once Fin resolutions are added, how accurate Fin is on your own help content, and how support responds. We have not paid for a plan.

    Which teammates need a paid seat?

    Everyone who talks to customers. The pricing FAQ (read 17 November 2026) says a Full seat is paid and required for teammates who interact with customers and use core features, and that only Full seats can send messages or fully use outbound content. Lite seats are free on Advanced (20 included) and Expert (50) and are meant for internal collaboration. The minimum is one Full seat, with no maximum.

    Can I downgrade or remove seats in the middle of a term?

    You can change plans, seats and add-ons yourself in the workspace, or via Sales if you are under contract, but the pricing FAQ (read 17 November 2026) says downgrades typically take effect at renewal. On an annual plan, assume you pay for the higher plan until the year ends.
