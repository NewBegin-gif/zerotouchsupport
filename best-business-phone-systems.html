1. Quo — Startups that want a modern, app-first number

Shared business numbers, texting and AI call summaries for small teams, formerly OpenPhone. Call reliability off Wi-Fi is the recurring complaint, and there is no video conferencing.

My take: The former OpenPhone — the cleanest app of the bunch, with AI call summaries that actually save time. Read our full Quo review →

2. CallHippo — Cheap international numbers, fast setup

AI-driven business phone system. Virtual numbers in 50+ countries, power dialer, call analytics and AI agents — set up your sales and support calling in minutes.

My take: Broadest country coverage at entry pricing; the AI dialer is a real plus for outbound teams. Read our full CallHippo review →

Visit CallHippo →

3. Aircall — Support & sales teams living in their CRM

Cloud phone system for sales and support teams: call routing, analytics and CRM integrations.

My take: The most mature integrations — if your team works out of HubSpot or Pipedrive, this just clicks. Read our full Aircall review →

Visit Aircall →

4. CloudTalk — Call centers that need smart routing

Cloud call centre with call routing, analytics and CRM integrations, priced per user; the Expert tier needs at least three licences.

My take: Strong analytics and routing; built for higher call volumes rather than solo founders. Read our full CloudTalk review →

Visit CloudTalk →

5. KrispCall — Solo founders on a budget

Virtual numbers across 100+ countries with a shared dashboard, dialer and CRM hooks, priced per user.

My take: The most affordable way to look professional with a dedicated business number. Read our full KrispCall review →

Visit KrispCall →

6. Calilio — Simple, no-nonsense cloud calling

Cloud phone system with international numbers and call menus, priced per user.

My take: Light, friendly and fast to set up — the simplicity is the point, and the pricing page says so plainly. Read our full Calilio review →

7. Vida AI — Letting AI answer your phone entirely

Deploy autonomous frontline voice agents. Let AI answer calls, book appointments, and handle FAQs 24/7 without sounding robotic.
