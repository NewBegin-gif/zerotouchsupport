1. Quo — Startups that want a modern, app-first number

Shared business numbers, texting and AI call summaries for small teams, formerly OpenPhone. About $15/user/month on annual billing, plus telecom taxes and surcharges in 16 US states. Call reliability off Wi-Fi is the recurring complaint, and there is no video conferencing.

My take: The former OpenPhone — the cleanest app of the bunch, with AI call summaries that actually save time. Read my hands-on review →

Visit Quo →

2. CallHippo — Cheap international numbers, fast setup

AI-driven business phone system. Virtual numbers in 50+ countries, power dialer, call analytics and AI agents — set up your sales and support calling in minutes. Its omnichannel inbox threads voice, WhatsApp, SMS, email, Instagram and Telegram into one conversation per customer.

My take: Broadest country coverage at entry pricing; the AI dialer is a real plus for outbound teams. Read our full CallHippo review →

Visit CallHippo →

3. Aircall — Support & sales teams living in their CRM

Cloud phone system for sales and support teams: call routing, analytics and CRM integrations. A three-licence minimum applies, so entry is effectively about $90/month; Essentials is roughly $30/user/month annual against $40 month-to-month.

My take: The most mature integrations — if your team works out of HubSpot or Pipedrive, this just clicks. Read our full Aircall review →

Visit Aircall →

4. CloudTalk — Call centers that need smart routing

Cloud call centre with call routing, analytics and CRM integrations, from €19/user/month on annual billing (Expert €49 needs at least three licences). AI voice agents are separate subscriptions from €99/month for 200 minutes, and number rental plus outbound minutes stack on top of the seat price.

My take: Strong analytics and routing; built for higher call volumes rather than solo founders. Read our full CloudTalk review →

Visit CloudTalk →

5. KrispCall — Solo founders on a budget

Virtual numbers across 100+ countries with a shared dashboard, dialer and CRM hooks, from about $12/user/month on annual billing. Calls and SMS are pay-as-you-go from a separate credit balance, so compare per-country call rates at your volume rather than the seat price.

My take: The most affordable way to look professional with a dedicated business number. Read my hands-on review →

Visit KrispCall →

6. Calilio — Simple, no-nonsense cloud calling

Cloud phone system with international numbers and call menus, from about $12/user/month annual on Standard. Numbers, minutes and SMS meter separately from a credit balance, so an active team's real bill sits above the per-seat price.

My take: Light, friendly and fast to set up — I reviewed it hands-on and the simplicity is the point. Read my hands-on review →

Visit Calilio →

7. Vida AI — Letting AI answer your phone entirely

Deploy autonomous frontline voice agents. Let AI answer calls, book appointments, and handle FAQs 24/7 without sounding robotic.

My take: Not a phone system but an AI receptionist — pair it with any of the above and stop missing calls. Read my hands-on review →

Visit Vida AI →
