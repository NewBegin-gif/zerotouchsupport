Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

Free plan to start; Essential $39 per agent/mo, Growth $89 (recommended), Professional $119, Enterprise custom — all billed annually, with monthly billing 20% higher ($49 against $39 on Essential). The AI Agent is included on every paid tier, but its automated resolutions bill separately at $0.99 per outcome: you pay when it actually resolves a query, not per attempt. eDesk claims the agent can automate up to 65% of support across channels, so model the outcome fee against your ticket volume — at 1,000 resolved tickets a month that is $990 on top of the seats. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The per-agent price is the entry ticket, but the AI Agent charges $0.99 for every automated resolution on top. That is great when it deflects real tickets, but it is a variable line item that scales with your support volume — model it against your ticket count before assuming a flat bill. And the honest fundamental for any e-commerce helpdesk: the value is only as good as your marketplace integrations actually syncing — test Amazon/eBay/Shopify connections with your real accounts during the free trial.

What buyers report about eDesk

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run eDesk ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It is built for marketplace sellers rather than adapted to them, and reviewers integrating multiple storefronts say that shows.
  • • Support during setup is repeatedly called quick and helpful. Around 4.4 to 4.5 on G2.
What they complain about
  • • Annual price increases without clear advance notice is the loudest and most repeated complaint — reviewers describe it happening year after year, including to customers who were promised held pricing.
  • • Downgrading is not self-service. You have to ask, which matters when the price has just gone up.
  • • Support response times are inconsistent once you are past onboarding.
  • • It is priced per agent per month, so a small team is a real line item rather than a rounding error.

When the paid plan is worth it

eDesk's entry-level Essential plan ($39 per user/month billed annually, $49 monthly) covers a seller running a single online store or marketplace, with centralized messaging, live chat, and rule-based automation included. The concrete threshold is the store/marketplace count: Essential caps out at 1 connected channel. So anyone selling on multiple marketplaces — Amazon plus a Shopify store, say — is pushed to Growth (5 stores, $89/user/month annual) or Professional (10 stores, $119/user/month annual, plus 2 free read-only users).

What upgrading does not solve is the AI Agent cost: eDesk charges $0.99 per resolved outcome on every plan, including Enterprise. Moving up tiers adds seats and channel capacity but does not reduce or cap the per-resolution AI fee, which scales linearly with support volume regardless of subscription level. API access is also gated separately: it requires the custom-priced Enterprise plan, not just a higher per-user tier.

How to actually use eDesk

Getting first value from eDesk means connecting at least one channel through the App Store — a marketplace like Amazon or eBay, or a webstore like Shopify. Browse the channel tiles, click Install App, then complete that channel's authorization flow so its messages start flowing into the eDesk Mailbox. This requires an Admin login; team members without Admin rights have to request access before they can add anything. eDesk offers a 14-day full-feature free trial with no credit card required, so testing channel connections costs nothing upfront.

Frequently asked questions

What does eDesk cost per agent?

$39 per agent a month on Essential billed annually ($49 monthly), $89 on Growth, $119 on Professional, with Enterprise quoted. Annual billing saves 20%. Read on 17 August 2026.

How does the $0.99 AI charge actually work?

It is per resolved outcome, not per attempt or per message: if the agent answers a 'where is my order' query end to end, that is $0.99. The AI Agent itself is included on every paid tier. The honest way to budget it is against your own deflection rate — eDesk claims up to 65% automation, and at a thousand resolved tickets a month the fee alone is $990.

Is the free plan enough to evaluate it?

It is enough to see the shared inbox and the channel integrations, which is the core of the product. What it will not tell you is the thing that decides the bill: how many of your tickets the AI Agent can close on its own. Run a paid month against real volume before committing to a year.

What should I weigh before buying Communication & Voice software?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.
