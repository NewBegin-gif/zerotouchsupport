Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 3 Nov 2026

Orateur prices by plan, each with an allowance of AI agent time. Creator costs CHF 49 a month with 4 hours of agent time, 1 user, a free phone number, call history and analytics, and email support; extra minutes cost CHF 0.24. Pro costs CHF 99 a month with 9 hours, 2 users and priority support; extra minutes CHF 0.22. Scale costs CHF 199 a month with 19 hours and 5 users; extra minutes CHF 0.20.

Enterprise is custom priced through sales. Paying annually gives two months free, according to the pricing toggle; we did not see the annual totals written out. You start with 100 free minutes and no commitment. The page has a cost calculator: at 500 minutes a month it recommends Pro, and shows Creator at CHF 111.40 once the extra minutes are added. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

The honest knock

The public pricing page shows Swiss francs in French and pounds in English (read 10 November 2026, pounds again on 17 November). Inside the app, in our own test session on 17 November 2026, the billing page showed the plans in US dollars: Creator $49, Pro $99 and Scale $199 a month, or $490, $990 and $1,990 a year. Orateur also says different things about languages. The pricing FAQ, read again on 17 November 2026, still says the AI supports English and French; the FAQ on its affiliate page says the service is available in English, French, German and Italian; and in our test session the receptionist settings offered a voice and greeting per language for French, English, German, Italian, Hebrew and Arabic, next to a region setting for the United Kingdom, France and the French, German and Italian parts of Switzerland.

We only switched languages on in the settings and did not hold a call in any language, so we cannot say how well the AI handles German, Italian, Hebrew or Arabic callers. The pricing FAQ also claims end-to-end encrypted calls and SOC 2 Type II compliance; we have not seen a report. Calendar sync is named for Google Calendar and Outlook only.

What Orateur does

AI phone receptionist for small businesses in Switzerland and French-speaking Europe: answers calls around the clock, books appointments in Google Calendar or Outlook, takes messages and transfers calls.

Pick the plan by call minutes

The plans differ mainly in included minutes and users, so count your monthly call minutes first. Creator covers 240 minutes; above roughly 450 minutes a month Pro (540 minutes) is cheaper than Creator plus extra minutes, as the calculator on the pricing page shows. Scale only makes sense above about 1,000 minutes or when you need more than 2 users.

    How to actually use Orateur

    Start the free trial on orateur.ai; you get 100 free minutes and no commitment. You can first hear it work: the homepage has a demo call, or you can let the agent call you on a Swiss, French or UK number. In the app, the receptionist settings have a tab per language (French, English, German, Italian, Hebrew and Arabic in our own test session on 17 November 2026), each with its own voice and first message; French was the default.

    Before you go live, place a test call in every language your callers use, because we did not test calls in any language. Each plan includes a free phone number. Orateur reads your availability and books, moves or cancels appointments in Google Calendar or Outlook, takes messages with the caller's name, number and reason, transfers calls to you when needed, and answers questions about your hours, prices and services. There is also an iOS app.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Orateur?

    Exit check 2026-11-17; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does Orateur cost?

    Read on 3 November 2026: Creator CHF 49 a month (4 hours of AI agent time), Pro CHF 99 (9 hours) and Scale CHF 199 (19 hours), plus custom Enterprise pricing. Extra minutes cost CHF 0.20 to 0.24, and annual billing gives two months free.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes. You start with 100 free minutes and no commitment, and the homepage offers a demo call.

    What can the AI receptionist do?

    Orateur answers every call around the clock, books, moves or cancels appointments in your calendar, takes detailed messages, transfers important calls to you, and answers questions about hours, prices and services.

    Which languages does Orateur speak?

    Orateur's pricing FAQ, read on 17 November 2026, says English and French, with automatic detection of the caller's language. The FAQ on its affiliate page says English, French, German and Italian, and in our own test session on 17 November 2026 the receptionist settings offered French, English, German, Italian, Hebrew and Arabic. We switched languages on but did not hold a call, so we have not confirmed how the AI handles any of them; test the ones your callers use during the 100 free minutes.

    Which calendars does it work with?

    The homepage names Google Calendar and Outlook.

    Do I get a phone number?

    Each plan includes a free phone number.

    Can I test it before connecting my phone number?

    Yes. The FAQ on orateur.ai/pricing, read 10 November 2026, says the 100 free minutes come with full access and that you can test the AI in your browser before connecting a phone number. Whether the trial asks for a card is not confirmed. One running cost to know before you go live: every SMS notification, sent when an appointment is booked, moved or cancelled, costs 30 credits, and the same FAQ puts one credit at roughly one second of call time, so each text costs about as much as half a minute of calling.

    What happens to my phone number if I cancel?

    The FAQ (read 10 November 2026) says the number stays reserved for 30 days after the subscription ends and keeps answering calls while you still have credits. Resubscribe inside those 30 days and you keep it; after that it is released and cannot be recovered. If the number is printed on your cards or website, move callers to another number before you cancel.
