Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 10 Sep 2026

(2026-09-10, read on their own pricing page) No seat price is published. Three plans are listed — Professional, Single-Line PowerDialer and Multi-Line PowerDialer — and no amount is rendered anywhere on the page; the billing toggle that used to sit above the cards has gone too, so there is now no path from this page to a number. The core phone system is identical on all three; what separates them is dialling.

There is no PowerDialer on Professional, one line on Single-Line, and up to four simultaneous lines on Multi-Line — also the only tier offering AI human voice detection, as a premium add-on. ConnectionBoost, Conversation Intelligence and DNC compliance are add-ons at every tier. The plan cards claim unlimited US/Canada minutes while the FAQ published in the page's own structured data prices unlimited US and Canada minutes at $30 a month per user, so which of the two applies belongs in writing in your quote.

First US/Canada number free, extra local numbers $5.00 a month, porting in free. Texts from $0.011 each ($11 per 1,000) on a registered 10DLC number, inbound free. 7-day trial, no credit card. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The dialer itself is genuinely fast and CRM-native; just buy it with the full basket priced and the calendar reminder set (our Renewal Radar exists for exactly this).

What Kixie does

Power dialer with SMS and CRM integration for outbound sales teams. Kixie publishes no seat price: three tiers separated by how many lines they dial, with the multi-line AI voice detection, ConnectionBoost, Conversation Intelligence and DNC compliance all sold as add-ons on top.

When the paid plan is worth it

Kixie no longer publishes a seat price. The pricing page lists three tiers — Professional, Single-Line PowerDialer and Multi-Line PowerDialer — with a quarterly and yearly toggle but no amount rendered anywhere in the page, so the only honest comparison is what each tier contains. The core phone system is identical across all three: business phone service, bi-directional CRM integration, texting, click-to-call, live call coaching, voicemail drop, IVR, ring groups, call queues, call recording, rule-based routing and the developer API.

What you are actually paying up for is dialling. Single-Line adds one dialler line and calling session management; Multi-Line dials up to four lines at once and is the only tier where AI human voice detection is available, and that is itself a premium add-on rather than an inclusion. So upgrading buys throughput, not features, and the add-ons you were sold on stay separate lines whichever tier you land on.

How to actually use Kixie

The trial is seven days, needs no credit card, and their own claim is that you are running in about two minutes. Two practical points before you start. Numbers: your first US or Canada number is free, extra local numbers are $5.00 a month, and existing numbers can be ported in free of charge. The vendor says it handles the transfer with no service interruption, and that ported numbers stay yours if you leave.

Minutes: the plan cards say unlimited minutes covering US and Canada calls, while the FAQ prices unlimited US and Canada minutes at $30 a month per user. Establish in writing which of the two applies to the quote you are given before you count seats. Outside those countries minutes bill at current rates. Texts are $0.011 each to US and Canada numbers on a registered 10DLC number, and inbound texts are free. Connect the CRM early: HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive and Zapier are the named integrations.

Distilled from Vendor pricing page including its plan comparison table and the FAQ block published in the page's own structured data, which carries the trial terms, number costs, minute bundle and text rates (kixie.com/pricing), read 18 August 2026. The vendor support centre at support.kixie.com returned HTTP 403 and could not be read.

The natural comparison is PhoneBurner or JustCall — dialer peers with simpler pricing — compare all-in per-seat including minutes, and the contract terms.
