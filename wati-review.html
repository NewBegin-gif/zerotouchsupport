Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 14 Aug 2026

Growth €59/mo, Pro €119/mo and Business €279/mo billed annually, with a 7-day free trial and no setup fee. Users are capped per tier: Growth includes 3 and lets you add none at all; Pro and Business include 5, with extra seats at €39 and €89 per user per month. Add-ons are separate again — 1,000 automation triggers €35/mo, 1,000 AI co-pilot credits €25/mo, Shopify $4.99/mo, each extra WhatsApp number €29/mo.

Our dollar reading of the same page on 23 July 2026 gave Growth $39, Pro $79 and Business $199 annually, so which currency and which figure you see depends on where Wati places you — check your own checkout before you budget. On top of all of it sit Meta's own per-conversation message fees, which Wati bills through at its own rate card. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: Our earlier text put extra Business seats at $59 per user per month. That figure is wrong: our own monthly captures of wati.io/pricing in July and August 2026 both carry $24 and $69 for the two extra-seat lines, and the euro storefront on 14 August 2026 shows €39 and €89 in the same two places. Read Pro extra seats as $24 and Business extra seats as $69. The add-on blocks read €35 for 1,000 automation triggers and €25 for 1,000 AI co-pilot credits on that euro page, against the $25 and $20 we recorded in dollars on 23 July 2026. That is not a like-for-like comparison, so we are not calling it a rise either. (re-verified 2026-08-14)

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Growth is capped at three users and you cannot buy a fourth at any price, so growing past three people forces the jump to Pro; from there extra seats are $24 each, and $69 each on Business. Automation triggers, AI credits, the Shopify link and every additional WhatsApp number are all separately metered, and Meta's per-conversation fees land on top of the lot. Model your real message volume and seat count before committing — that is where the money actually goes.

What Wati does

WhatsApp Business API platform for sales and support: shared team inbox, bulk broadcasts and no-code chatbots — plus Astra, its AI agent builder for agents that actually resolve conversations (orders, refunds, bookings, lead qualification) across WhatsApp, web and voice. Its omnichannel inbox now also carries TikTok DMs natively (all plans, no extra cost) alongside Instagram and RCS — with ad-to-chat context that shows exactly which TikTok ad triggered a conversation. It now ships its own MCP server as well: connect Claude or ChatGPT once and manage contacts, send WhatsApp campaigns, read past chats and build agents in plain language, while sends still run on your own approved templates and the full history stays in the Wati dashboard. Wati's own page puts the cost like this: free for current paid Astra customers with no usage cap during launch, and full MCP access on every Astra plan for new customers (checked 4 Aug 2026).

What buyers report about Wati

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026 (Shopify App Store reviews from November 2025 through 7 July 2026). We have not run Wati ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Ease of use is mentioned by over 100 reviewers, automation by 71. 4.6 on both G2 (482 reviews) and Capterra (203).
  • • Shared inbox, no-code chatbot and broadcasts genuinely cut the repeat questions about price, delivery and order status.
  • • Shopify merchants running the cart-recovery app report an uptick in sales after wiring it up, and name the link between Facebook ads and WhatsApp messaging as the best part. The AI agent is credited with answering fast and pointing customers at the right products.
  • • Order confirmations no longer going out by hand, and customer segmentation that one merchant says finally brought sanity to their database.
What they complain about
  • • Wati adds roughly 20% on top of Meta's own per-template message rates. On 3,000 marketing messages that is about $90 a month extra; on 10,000, about $300. The plan price is not your bill — and on 14 August 2026 that plan price came to us in euros, €59 to €279 a month, not dollars.
  • • Expense and cost are the two most-cited negatives (39 and 32 mentions), with the markup structure named separately 23 times.
  • • Support is the single most common complaint across all three platforms — non-priority tickets run one to three business days.
  • • Billing is where Shopify merchants get stuck. One paying customer sat in a dunning block for over a week after three different bank cards failed on Wati's own payment gateway — their bank confirmed nothing was wrong at its end — and support would not lift it, which killed the mobile app entirely in the meantime.
  • • A Growth-plan store could no longer send campaign broadcasts after a policy change and was told only that it was policy. Another describes unexpected charges around a trial that runs just 7 days.
  • • Support complaints repeat here as they do elsewhere: days without a reply, then a request for generic photos at 2am; an annual-plan store that got only automated email updates and no phone help.

Where the ratings disagree. The Shopify App Store splits into two camps with almost nothing in between: merchants who are unreserved about the AI and the automation, and merchants hit by billing or support failures. That is sharper than the G2 and Capterra picture, and the likely reason is timing — Shopify sellers write their review at the moment money moves, so a failed card or a blocked broadcast lands in the review rather than in a support ticket. Note also that the earlier plan-price range in our own notes, 59 to 279, is the euro storefront we were served on 14 August 2026, not dollars.

When the paid plan is worth it

Growth ($39/mo annual) pays for itself once WhatsApp is a channel you answer every day and three seats genuinely cover the team. But Growth sells no extra seats at any price. So if you are already three people, price Pro ($79/mo plus $24 per extra seat) from day one and save yourself the forced mid-quarter jump. Business ($199/mo) only earns its premium once you concretely need multiple WhatsApp numbers or the advanced AI agents.

And know what upgrading does not fix: Meta's per-conversation fees are identical on every tier, so the metered part of the bill never shrinks by paying Wati more.

How to actually use Wati

Setup is three chains, not one: a verified Meta Business Manager account (have real business documents ready), a phone number that is not already tied to any WhatsApp account, and then Wati's own connect flow — which is genuinely quick once the first two exist. The comments under every tutorial tell the same story: people rarely get stuck in Wati itself, they get stuck at Meta's business verification and at numbers that will not release from an existing WhatsApp.

Budget days, not minutes, for Meta's checks; then start small with one approved template broadcast and the no-code chatbot builder before layering automations.

The natural comparison is Interakt or a direct Meta BSP — other WhatsApp API providers.

Frequently asked questions

What does an extra user on Wati cost?

It depends which tier, and Growth is the trap: it includes 3 users and sells no fourth at any price, so a fourth person forces the jump to Pro. On the dollar page we captured in July and August 2026 the two extra-seat lines read $24 and $69 per user per month; on the euro page we were served on 14 August 2026 they read €39 and €89. Pro is the cheaper seat, Business the expensive one.

Am I being quoted in the right currency?

Worth checking before you budget. Our July and August 2026 captures of wati.io/pricing came back in dollars; on 14 August 2026 the same URL served us euros — Growth €59, Pro €119, Business €279 a month on annual billing — while the Shopify add-on stayed at $4.99. We cannot see from here what drives that, so take the figure from your own checkout, in your own currency, rather than from anyone's summary including ours.

Does paying Wati more reduce the per-message cost?

No. Meta's per-conversation fees are charged the same on every tier and Wati bills them through on its own rate card, so the metered half of the bill does not shrink when you upgrade. Upgrading buys seats, automation triggers, AI credits and extra WhatsApp numbers — and those are separately metered too.

What should I weigh before buying Communication & Voice software?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.
