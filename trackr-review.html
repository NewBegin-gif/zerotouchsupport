Trackr Review (2026)

Customer SupportResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-12

The rating is 4.1 over 56 reviews on 12 September 2026, and the shape says more than the average: 45 five-star, three three-star, one two-star and seven one-star, which is a split verdict rather than a soft middle.

Best for: Shopify stores shipping a few hundred to a few thousand parcels a month that want where-is-my-order traffic off the support desk — one branded tracking page across every carrier, estimated delivery dates, and proactive email, WhatsApp or US SMS updates — and that like the idea of tracking living in Apple and Google Wallet instead of in an email thread.
Who should skip it: if you ship under a hundred parcels a month, where $10 buys 400 shipments you will not use and the carrier's own page is free; you need SMS outside the United States, which is not offered; or you cannot carry the risk of a tracking page that went down inside the last year.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 12 Sep 2026

Four tiers and no free plan, billed in USD every 30 days through Shopify: Basic $10 a month or $96 a year for 400 shipments a month, Standard $45 or $432 for 2,000, Advanced $150 or $1,440 for 9,000 and Enterprise $250 or $2,400 for 18,000. Annual billing saves 20% and every tier carries a 14-day trial. The feature list is identical on all four — branded tracking page, estimated delivery dates, email, WhatsApp and US-only SMS updates, Apple and Google Wallet tracking, Klaviyo, Omnisend, Segment and chatbot integrations, product upsell and translation — so the only thing the ladder sells is shipment volume.

The Shopify listing does not say what happens past your allowance. The vendor's own page does: parceltrackr.com/pricing, read the same day, prints $0.05 per extra shipment on every tier. That number decides whether you upgrade or overrun, because in-plan a shipment costs 2.5 cents on Basic, 2.25 on Standard, 1.7 on Advanced and 1.4 on Enterprise — so an overrun is priced at two to four times what the same shipment costs inside the plan.

The two sources also disagree about the trial: the listing offers 3,000 and 5,000 bonus trackings on Advanced and Enterprise, the vendor page 1,500 and 2,000. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The reviews name the reason. On 14 June 2026 a merchant wrote that the app was down again and asked for it to be fixed; the vendor replied the next day confirming a service disruption that had since been resolved. For an app whose entire job is the page a customer opens when they are already worried about a parcel, downtime is the failure mode, not an inconvenience. The listing is also careless with its own numbers: it says more than 1,900 carriers in one line and more than 2,200 in another, while the vendor's site says 2,100-plus.

And one advertised feature deserves a straight look before you switch it on — a dropshipping mode that hides the carrier and the Chinese origin of a shipment from your customer. The listing presents it as a selling point; whether your tracking page should withhold that is a decision about your own store, and worth making deliberately.

What Trackr does

Shopify order-tracking app that replaces carrier links with one branded tracking page: estimated delivery dates, email, WhatsApp and US SMS updates, Apple and Google Wallet tracking, 2,100-plus carriers, shipping analytics and product recommendations on the tracking page. No free plan — Basic $10 a month or $96 a year for 400 shipments, Standard $45 or $432 for 2,000, Advanced $150 or $1,440 for 9,000, Enterprise $250 or $2,400 for 18,000, and $0.05 for every shipment above the allowance. When the paid plan is worth it

Four tiers and a single variable: shipment volume. Basic is $10 a month for 400 shipments, Standard $45 for 2,000, Advanced $150 for 9,000 and Enterprise $250 for 18,000, with annual billing 20% off and a 14-day trial on each. The feature list is identical on all four: the ladder sells headroom and nothing else. That makes the overage the number to know, and the Shopify listing does not print it.

The vendor's pricing page does: $0.05 per extra shipment on every tier. Moving up is the cheaper correction whenever an overrun is more than marginal. What paying more does not buy: SMS remains United States only on every tier, and no plan carries a feature the others lack -- the vendor's own "every plan comes with" list covers carriers, notifications, integrations and analytics without distinction. The two sources also disagree on the trial bonus -- the listing offers 3,000 and 5,000 extra trackings on Advanced and Enterprise, the vendor page 1,500 and 2,000.

How to actually use Trackr

Nothing tracks until an order is fulfilled and a tracking number is attached to it, by hand or by your fulfilment provider. That step registers the shipment and starts pulling carrier scans. The page itself sits at your-store.myshopify.com/apps/trackorder. To put it in your navigation, open Online Store then Navigation in the Shopify admin, pick a menu, add a menu item and type /apps/trackorder into the link field; the app's preferences page has a shortcut button for the same thing.

The proxy prefix is editable under Apps, then About next to Trackr, then App proxy -- but change it and the menu link has to change to match. Turn on notifications under Settings, Automatic email notifications: customers are mailed on Ordered, In Transit, Out for delivery and Delivered, with intermediary carrier updates batched once every 24 hours. One detail decides how fast the plan drains: each unique tracking number spends one shipment from the allowance, while customer lookups and repeat status mails do not.

Distilled from The setup steps, the /apps/trackorder proxy path, the app-proxy customisation and the notification statuses come from Trackr's own FAQ, https://parceltrackr.com/faq/ (read 12 September 2026), which is also where the allowance rule is stated -- each unique tracking number consumes plan resources while repeat customer lookups and status mails do not. Plans, the $0.05 per extra shipment and the trial bonuses were read the same day on https://parceltrackr.com/pricing/ and cross-checked against the Shopify listing, https://apps.shopify.com/trackr, which prints different trial bonuses and no overage rate.

The natural comparison is the carrier's tracking page and Shopify's own order status page — Both are free, both already work, and that is the bar this app has to clear. Shopify emails a shipping confirmation with a carrier link and the carrier shows the scans. What $10 a month buys is the part those two do not do: one branded page across every carrier you use, an estimated delivery date, updates pushed over WhatsApp or US SMS before the customer asks, and tracking on the phone's lock screen through Apple and Google Wallet. If where-is-my-order messages are rare, the free pair is enough. The app starts paying for itself at the point where someone on your side answers that question several times a day.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Customer Support software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
