Process Street: turn your support SOPs into checklists the team actually follows

Most "zero-touch" failures aren't about the tools — they're about consistency. The how-to for handling a refund, escalating an angry ticket, or running a QA check lives in a Google Doc nobody opens after week one. So every agent does it slightly differently, edge cases get missed, and the same mistakes resurface. Documentation that just sits there doesn't standardise anything.

The fix is to turn those procedures from something you read into something you run. That's the whole idea behind Process Street, and it's the quiet backbone that makes the rest of a support stack reliable.

What is Process Street?

Process Street is a process-management platform that converts text-heavy documentation into active, checklist-driven workflows. Instead of reading how to onboard a customer or audit a support ticket, your team runs a live instance of that process — tracked step by step, with clear accountability for each stage.

Why it underpins a zero-touch support stack

Consistency is the lever that makes everything else (AI agents, shared inboxes, voice automation) trustworthy. Process Street builds that consistency in a few concrete ways:

  • Shared, assignable workflows: bring the whole team into the same process and hand specific steps to specific people, so support execution isn't trapped in one person's head.
  • Conditional logic: the checklist adapts to what's entered — flag a ticket "high priority" and the right approval steps and notifications appear automatically.
  • AI-built templates: spin up a full, structured procedure from a short prompt, so writing the SOPs themselves stops being the bottleneck.
  • A clear audit trail: every run logs who did which check, what they entered, and when — invaluable when you need to see why something went wrong.

When a procedure becomes something the team actively runs rather than something they're supposed to remember, the routine errors quietly disappear — which is exactly what lets the more automated parts of your support run unattended.
