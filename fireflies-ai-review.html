Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 31 Aug 2026

Free $0 — unlimited transcription, limited AI summaries, 400 mins storage per team, 20 AI credits. Pro $18/seat/mo month-to-month or $10/seat/mo billed annually — unlimited AI summaries, 8,000 mins storage per seat, 20 AI credits. Business $29/seat/mo month-to-month or $19/seat/mo billed annually — unlimited storage, 30 AI credits. Enterprise $39/seat/mo, annual only — 50 AI credits, SSO/SCIM, HIPAA, private storage. CORRECTION to the previous dossier: $10 and $19 were carried as the price; they are the annual rates and month-to-month is 80% and 53% higher.

Enterprise was missing entirely. The AI-credit ladder (20/20/30/50) means Pro adds no AI credits over the free plan. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

And AI credits are capped on every tier — Pro carries the same 20 a month as the free plan, so upgrading to Pro buys recording and storage rather than more AI. If you mainly want real-time in-Zoom captioning and simple English notes, Otter is the simpler pick.

What Fireflies.ai does

AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes and summarizes your calls across Zoom, Google Meet and Teams — searchable notes, action items and CRM sync, so nobody takes minutes by hand.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan is the unusual part: transcription is unlimited and it is the AI layer that is metered. So the question is never whether you can afford to record your meetings — you can, at zero — but whether the summarising and search layer earns its seat price. Read the headline numbers carefully, because they are the annual rates. Pro is $10 per seat a month billed annually and $18 month-to-month; Business is $19 annually and $29 month-to-month; Enterprise is $39 per seat and annual only.

Ten people on Business is $190 a month on an annual commitment and $290 without one — a difference no feature comparison shows you. What upgrading does not fix is AI credits, and Pro is where that bites: Free and Pro both carry 20 a month. Paying for Pro buys video recording, downloads and 8,000 minutes of storage a seat — not more AI processing. Credits rise only at Business (30) and Enterprise (50), so if AI volume is your constraint, Pro is not the answer to it.

How to actually use Fireflies.ai

First value is a transcript, and the fastest route is the calendar connection rather than the app: connect Google or Microsoft calendar and Fireflies joins scheduled meetings as a participant. For one-off calls, invite the notetaker directly. Before you roll it out to a team, count AI credits rather than seats. Free and Pro both include 20 a month, so a Pro rollout buys recording, downloads and storage but no additional AI processing.

Track how many meetings actually get summarised in month one — that number, not headcount, tells you whether Business is warranted. Check storage against the same reality: Free gives 400 minutes for the whole team, Pro 8,000 minutes per seat, Business unlimited. Then decide where transcripts land. One that arrives in the CRM gets read; one that stays in Fireflies does not. The vendor's guide covers the integrations and the troubleshooting path for failed transcriptions.
