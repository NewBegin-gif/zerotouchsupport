We tested it · 2026-11-16

The app was installed on our Shopify development store, which we set up, and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. On 16 November 2026 the session tested Cloze, the Shopify AI chat app (not Cloze CRM), on the Free plan of a Shopify development store: the install, syncing products, one shopper question in the editor preview, the three price lists, the plan screens up to the Shopify approval screen and the uninstall dialog. Nothing was paid, approved or uninstalled. The app overview step 2 showed 0 / 18 products synced with a button Review sources, which opened AI Training, tab Knowledge, with the text No products have been synced from the connected store yet. Sync products now gave the toast products sync queued; after 15 seconds the list was still at 0 and after a reload it read Products (16) with Last synced Nov 16, 01:07 PM UTC. That is 16 products and not 18; the reason was not looked into. Policies, FAQs, pages, orders and collections were all (0). Step 1, Enable in theme, opened the theme editor (app embeds) with Cloze Chat Widget off. We switched it on and in the preview a bar appeared at the bottom with What products do you recommend?. The theme change was not saved, was undone and the editor tab was closed, so the widget is not live in the theme. In the editor preview we asked How much is the 3p Fulfilled Snowboard and is it in stock? and the answer was The 3p Fulfilled Snowboard is priced at 2,629.95 USD and it is currently in stock. With a product card showing $2,629.95. The response took about 5 seconds, by eye and not timed. The Cloze product list says $2629.95 and 20 in stock and Shopify says 20 in stock; the price field in Shopify itself (the variant price) was not checked separately. The settings screens under AI Training, General showed Assistant role: Store guide, Response length: Balanced and Inventory visibility: Show only In Stock / Out of Stock; they were not tried further. Timing, approximate tester observations: from clicking Install at 14:06 to the answer at about 14:08, roughly 2 minutes in 6 steps (Install in the App Store, Install in the dialog, Sync products now, Enable in theme, switch the widget on, type and send the question). No email address, account or password was needed. Uninstall: the app menu offers Manage app, Pin to your navigation and Uninstall; Manage app opens the Shopify app page with View in Shopify App Store, Write review, View activity and Uninstall app; the Uninstall Cloze dialog (reason list, feedback box) was opened and cancelled. The app says a paid plan is changed or cancelled through Shopify; a cancellation of a subscription was not tested because there was no subscription. Not tested: a real payment, the trial, a plan change, the chatbot on the real storefront (the store has a password and the widget was only seen in the unsaved editor preview), policy and shipping questions, order status, human takeover, other languages, discounts, upsell, analytics, a catalogue above 100 products, the behaviour at the limit of 25 conversations, and contact with the developer. One AI conversation was used (24 of 25 left). No kit records were entered.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The vendor's own site sold it at $29 and $79, with unlimited conversations on both paid tiers. The screenshot below is that page. Two prices and two different meters for one product is not a discount you can claim, it is an inconsistency to ask about before you pay. The second is the track record, or the absence of one. The app launched 24 April 2026 and, as of 4 September 2026, carries exactly one review: five stars, left after eight days of use.

So there is next to no merchant experience to lean on, and the “Built for Shopify” badge is a technical bar, not a verdict on the answers. Also note what a conversation costs when it goes wrong. An assistant wired to live inventory and order tracking is answering with your data, and the free tier's 25 conversations a month is enough to test the tone but not enough to see it handle a bad day.

The listing promises a 3 day trial on paid plans (read 16 Nov 2026), which is short for a support tool, and the Starter approval screen in the app showed no trial on 16 Nov 2026, so ask before you rely on it.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is arithmetic in this pricing that is worth two minutes, because the overage rate is cheaper than the plans themselves. Every paid tier charges $0.10 for a conversation beyond the allowance. Inside the allowance you are paying $0.196 a conversation on Starter ($49 for 250), $0.132 on Pro ($99 for 750) and $0.0995 on Scale ($199 for 2,000). So the included conversations cost more than the extra ones on both of the lower tiers, and upgrading only starts to pay at a precise point.

Starter plus overage costs $49 plus ten cents a conversation; that equals Pro's $99 at exactly 750 conversations. Pro plus overage equals Scale's $199 at exactly 1,750. Below those two numbers, staying put and paying the overage is the cheaper choice, and the difference is real money at volume. What the tiers do buy outright is catalogue size and memory. Free syncs 100 products, Starter 1,000, Pro 10,000 and Scale unlimited, so a large catalogue can force a tier regardless of conversation volume.

History runs 7 days, 30, 90 and a year, which matters only if you plan to actually read the insights. Then there is the awkward part, and it is bigger than a discount. On 19 August 2026 the vendor's own site was selling this as Free with 50 conversations a month, Pro at $29 and Business at $79, and on that page the paid tiers advertise unlimited conversations, not an allowance.

The App Store listing our link lands on rations the same product at 250, 750 and 2,000 conversations for $49, $99 and $199. So the two pages differ on the price and on the meter itself, which makes the arithmetic above worth doing only if you install through the App Store. We cannot tell you which page is current, and the vendor does not explain the gap anywhere we could find.

Read both, and ask them directly which applies to a Shopify install before you pay for a tier.

    How to actually use Cloze

    Start on the free tier and read every one of the 25 conversations rather than counting them: what you are testing is whether the answers are right about your products, not whether the widget looks good. Feed it your worst cases first: the item with three near-identical variants, the policy question you get every week, the order that shipped late, because those are the conversations that cost you a customer when they go wrong.

    Set the human handoff up before you go live, not after. When you do move to a paid plan, count your likely monthly conversations first and compare staying on the lower tier with overage: Starter plus $0.10 a conversation only becomes more expensive than Pro past 750 conversations, and Pro only loses to Scale past 1,750. And before you pay anything, ask the vendor which page applies to a Shopify install: their own site quotes lower prices and unlimited conversations, the listing quotes higher prices and a monthly cap, and that answer changes the whole calculation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Cloze?

    Exit check 2026-11-16; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    What counts as a conversation, and do unused ones carry over?

    The FAQ on cloze.bot/faq (read 11 October 2026) defines a conversation as a unique chat session between the AI and a visitor, however many messages the visitor sends inside it, and says conversations reset monthly, so unused ones do not carry over. When a session ends and a new one starts is not stated: not confirmed. On the App Store plans each conversation beyond the allowance costs $0.10.

    Can a person take over a chat the AI cannot answer?

    Yes, on paid plans per the vendor. The cloze.bot FAQ (read 11 October 2026) says that on its Pro and Business plans you can enable human handoff, take over the conversation, reply and release it back to the AI. Those are the plan names on cloze.bot; the App Store listing you install from sells Starter, Pro and Scale and does not list handoff per tier, so which listing tier includes it is not confirmed.

    How do I cancel?

    From your Shopify admin under Apps, Cloze, Billing; the cloze.bot FAQ (read 11 October 2026) says there are no cancellation fees or long-term contracts. Charges on the App Store listing recur every 30 days.

    Is there a free trial?

    The App Store listing lists a 3 day free trial on Starter, Pro and Scale (read 16 Nov 2026), but the Shopify approval screen for Starter in the app on 16 Nov 2026 showed no trial, so confirm before you approve the charge. cloze.bot/pricing has Start Pro trial and Start Business trial buttons and does not state a length (read 16 Nov 2026).
