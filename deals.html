How we check offers

An offer appears under Extra benefits only when the vendor gave it to us or confirmed it: in writing, through a partner network, or on its own offer page. The card names which, and the date. We do not test codes at a checkout, so check the total before you pay.

Needs confirmation means the vendor has not confirmed or reconfirmed it. Conditional means the vendor grants it case by case. A missing end date means none was given, not that the offer runs forever; an offer with a stated end disappears from this page at that moment.

Free plans, trials and demos come from the vendor’s own description. Partner links add no discount. Some links are affiliate links: we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never sets the order: within each section the cards are alphabetical, and we never rank by the size of a discount, because 25% for three months can be worth less than 10% for a year.
