The cost-trap atlas for customer support and voice software

Researched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-24

43 tools in this category, 95 flags, and under every flag the line from our research dossier it rests on, written from the vendor’s own pricing page as we read it on the date shown.

A flag is not a complaint. It is the shape of the bill: what it is metered on, what sits outside the tier price, and what the headline rate assumes. Those are the things that decide what a tool costs you in a year, and they are rarely the number on the pricing page. We only flag what we can quote, because a flag without evidence is an opinion.

What the flags mean

MeteredThe bill follows usage, not headcount. Size it on your own volume, not on the entry price.
Per seatThe bill grows with the team. Multiply by the headcount you expect, not the one you have.
Free tierThere is a genuine free plan. Check what it leaves out before treating it as the answer.
Add-onsParts of what the marketing describes are sold separately from the tier price.
Quote onlyNo public rate. You cannot budget for it without a sales call.
AnnualThe headline rate assumes a year up front; the monthly figure is higher.
Renewal jumpThe first term is promotional and renews at a higher rate.

How we read a pricing page

Once a month we capture the vendor’s own pricing page and keep the file. The flags above come from our research dossier on those captures, and each one carries the sentence it rests on, so you can judge it yourself rather than take our word. Where a vendor publishes no rate at all, we say so; where our dossier has no comparable entry price, we point you to the pricing details instead of estimating.
