Helpdesk platforms: the entry tier is never the product you saw

Researched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-26

In this category the entry plan exists to be outgrown. Price the tier you will actually need.

Helpdesk vendors market the product at the top of the ladder and price the product at the bottom. That is not dishonest, but it does mean the demo you watched and the plan you costed are rarely the same thing — the routing, the AI and the reporting that made the demo compelling usually start two tiers up.

So the useful exercise is inverted. Instead of asking what the entry tier costs, list the three things that made you look for a helpdesk at all, find the cheapest tier at each vendor that includes all three, and compare those. The order often changes.

How we picked

  • Per-agent prices read on the vendor’s own page and dated, on annual billing where that is the default.
  • We say what the entry tier leaves out, since that is the number that decides your real cost.
  • We say who should skip each one — a shared inbox is a legitimate answer and is cheaper than all of these.

The picks

Zendesk
Best for: support teams past the point where a shared inbox works, who need routing, a help centre and reporting in one place and

The category default, and the entry tier is email and ticketing only. Everything people actually buy Zendesk for sits higher up the ladder, so costing it at the Support Team price will understate what you end up paying. Right when your volume genuinely needs routing and a help centre.

Skip it if you are a small team handling support out of email. The entry tier is email and ticketing only, the features people buy Zendesk for start at $55 a seat, and the Copilot AI is not available below $115. Also skip it if your case volume is low enough that per-resolution AI metering is more admin than benefit

Count channels before you count agents

The per-agent price is what everyone compares and the channel list is what actually decides. A one-channel tool at a low seat price is excellent value until a customer messages you on WhatsApp, at which point it is a migration. Write down every route a customer can reach you today, plus the one you expect to add, and disqualify on that first.

Then check the annual commitment. Most of the discounts in this category assume a year, which is a long time to be locked into a tool you have not yet run under real load.

Frequently asked questions

When does a shared inbox stop being enough?

When two people answer the same message, when something falls through because nobody owned it, or when you cannot answer ‘how long are we taking to reply’. Before that, a helpdesk mostly adds process.

Is the AI tier worth it?

It depends on how much of your volume is genuinely repetitive. If your top ten questions are most of your tickets, deflection pays quickly; if every ticket is different, you are buying a feature that has nothing to work with.

Can I move helpdesks later?

Tickets export, macros and workflows do not, and your team has to relearn a tool they use all day. Treat the choice as a two-to-three year one rather than an annual review.
