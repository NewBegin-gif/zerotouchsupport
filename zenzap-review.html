ZenZap Review (2026)Integrations Start at Business+

Communication & VoiceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

The pricing genuinely undercuts Slack — the honest question is ecosystem: team chat lives or dies on integrations and habit, and ZenZap's app catalog is a fraction of Slack's, so teams glued to hundreds of integrations will feel the gap. Throughout this review we weigh it against Slack.

Best for: small teams that run work over messy WhatsApp/personal chat today and want organized, professional chat with tasks — starting free.
Who should skip it: if your workflows depend on a deep integration ecosystem (Slack's moat), or you're already paid into Microsoft Teams via 365.
Price: Free plan; Pro $3/user/mo billed yearly ($4 monthly); Business+ $8/user yearly ($10 monthly) · full pricing as we read it on 1 Sep 2026 ↓

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Free plan: unlimited group chats, unlimited built-in to-dos, WhatsApp chat migration, AI voice-note transcription and in-chat search, 1 GB storage — the page publishes no user cap for it and no promise of unlimited seats. Pro $3/user/mo billed yearly ($4 monthly); Business+ $8/user yearly ($10 monthly); Enterprise custom. Flat-rate team bundles price below per-seat: Pro is $51/mo yearly for up to 20 users and $112 for up to 50, Business+ $89 and $190, quoted in dollars and pounds.

Yearly billing saves 33%. Integrations with business tools and API access start at Business+; audit logs, SSO and SAML/SCIM are Enterprise. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What buyers report about ZenZap

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run ZenZap ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Keeping work messaging out of personal WhatsApp is the whole point, and reviewers say it achieves that without becoming complicated.
  • • $3 per user on Pro, $8 on Business+, with a free tier at 1 GB and 5 project chats.
What they complain about
  • • Reviewers report service interruptions with no proactive communication, and support emails going unanswered. For a tool a team depends on daily, that is the risk.
  • • Notifications and file uploads are glitchy.
  • • Integrations are limited and sit on Business+ along with API access.

Integrations start at Business+, not Pro

Pro is $3 per user a month billed yearly ($4 monthly), Business+ $8 ($10 monthly) and Enterprise is custom; yearly billing saves 33%. Flat-rate team bundles undercut the per-seat price at every size the page lists — twenty users is $51 a month on Pro and $89 on Business+, against $60 and $160 per seat. The paid line is not where you would guess. Integrations with other business tools and API access begin at Business+, not Pro.

So "does it connect to the five tools we touch every day" is a question about the $8 tier, not the $3 one, and that changes the sum entirely. Pro buys voice and video calls, task templates and sub-tasks, admin and privacy controls and 100 GB of storage. The free plan is still a real landing spot: unlimited group chats, unlimited built-in to-dos, WhatsApp chat migration and 1 GB of storage.

The page publishes no user cap for it — but it does not promise unlimited seats either, so confirm that with the vendor before you plan a company-wide rollout on it. What Business+ does not buy: audit logs, SSO and SAML/SCIM sit above it, on Enterprise. If a security questionnaire asks for those, $8 a seat does not get you there.

How to actually use ZenZap

List the five integrations your team touches every day and check them by name — but check them against Business+ at $8 a user, because that is where integrations and API access begin. Pro at $3 does not carry them. Then run the free plan properly. Unlimited group chats, unlimited built-in to-dos and WhatsApp chat migration mean you can move one project or one department across and let habit decide rather than a trial clock.

Two things to confirm first: the pricing page publishes no user cap for the free tier but does not promise unlimited seats either, and free storage stops at 1 GB. Price the team bundle rather than the seat. At twenty users the flat rate is $51 a month on Pro against $60 per seat, and $89 on Business+ against $160. And keep Enterprise-shaped requirements out of the Business+ decision: audit logs, SSO and SAML/SCIM sit above it, so a compliance list containing those is a different conversation with a different price.
