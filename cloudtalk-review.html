The toggle promises 'save 3+ months (≈ 30% off)'; on those figures annual billing saves 25.6% on Essential, 26.5% on Starter, 29.0% on Expert and 29.6% on Lite, between three and four months of the monthly fee.

What buyers report about CloudTalk

Hands-on: not tested by usDocumented by the vendor: 4 features, read 2026-10-07Reported by buyers: G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and TrustRadius, read August 2026

Themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run CloudTalk ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Local numbers in 160+ countries is the single most-cited benefit, and for distributed sales teams it is the reason to pick it. 4.4 on G2 across 1,700+ reviews.
  • • Reporting and general usability get consistently good marks.
  • • TrustRadius reviewers keep landing on the same three things: the IVR builder is visual and quick to change, call recording and monitoring are dependable, and setup takes minutes rather than a project.
What they complain about
  • • Call quality and dropped calls are the top G2 theme at 176+ mentions, with connection issues at 81+. For a phone system that is the complaint that matters most.
  • • The $19/month headline is not the bill. Add the dialer, AI features and CRM access and reviewers report $45-55 per user per month.
  • • The iPhone app cannot transfer calls or send SMS and quits in the background; iOS trails desktop.
  • • Annual billing is rigid — monthly signups occasionally billed for twelve months, and no refunds on unused term. Read the term before you sign.
  • • Live phone support is gated to higher tiers, and chat runs 2-4 hours against an advertised 5-minute SLA.
  • • The specific gaps TrustRadius reviewers name are narrower than the G2 complaints and worth checking against your setup: three-way calling, features for physical IP phones, call summary notes, thin documentation, and an admin level between owner and ordinary operator.
  • • Onboarding, requesting phone numbers and initial training come up as the rough edges of an otherwise fast setup.

Where the ratings disagree. TrustRadius is the platform to read last, not first. Its CloudTalk reviews are almost uniformly enthusiastic, but the newest written one we could find dates from February 2023 — the sample is from before the AI voice agents and the current packaging existed. G2 and Trustpilot are noisier and much more current, which is where the call-quality and billing complaints live. Where they agree is the mobile app.

When the paid plan is worth it

CloudTalk has no free tier: the 14-day trial is the only unpaid route, so the real decision is which paid plan. On annual billing the ladder is Lite €19, Starter €25, Essential €29 and Expert €49 per user per month, and Expert carries a minimum of 3 licences — awkward for a two-person desk. The clearest hinge is outbound dialling: the Power Dialer is a €15 per user per month add-on on the lower plans and comes bundled with Expert, which absorbs much of the step up from Essential.

The Parallel Dialer stays an add-on at €39 per user per month. Upgrading the seat tier does not make the AI included. Conversation Intelligence renews at €9 per agent per month after a 30-day AI Analytics trial. AI voice agents are separate subscriptions: AI Receptionist from €99 per month, and AI Specialist €349 per month for 1,000 minutes. Above 10,000 minutes a month there is custom pricing, which CloudTalk advertises as low as €0.15 per minute.

Telephony stays separate too: CloudTalk prices a US local or mobile port at €30 per request plus €2 per number, and a US toll-free port at €30 per request plus €4 per number. CloudTalk told us on 31 August 2026 that porting fees and conditions differ per country and per customer, so read those published figures as the documented US case rather than a universal rate.

How to actually use CloudTalk

The 14-day trial includes 50 AI Voice Agent minutes, and buying a number is the fast path: CloudTalk documents at least one number type in 95 countries with no local address requirement or documentation. Where a number type does require documents, carrier verification can add 1-2 days to processing time. The help centre prices a US local or mobile port at €30 per request plus €2 per number, over 10-14 business days, and lists German national numbers at 37 business days.

It calls a name/address mismatch with the losing carrier the #1 cause of rejected ports, and requires the old line to stay active until the port completes. CloudTalk documents up to 100 kbps per call, calls 1 Mbps per person sharing the connection a good start, and asks you to exclude *.cloudtalk.io from firewall inspection on ports 80 and 443. CloudTalk Go requires Android 12 (API Level 31) or higher and at least 3 GB of RAM, and needs the 'Calling accounts' setting enabled or calls may not ring. It is not distributed via AppGallery, so Huawei users cannot install it.

Frequently asked questions

What does CloudTalk actually cost per user?

The seat is €19 to €49 a month on annual billing (Lite €19, Starter €25, Essential €29, Expert €49), and Expert has a three-licence minimum. The bill reviewers describe is higher than the headline because the dialers and AI are priced separately: Power Dialer €15 per user a month, Parallel Dialer €39, AI Conversation Intelligence €9. Power Dialer is already in Expert. Read on 16 August 2026.

How much are CloudTalk's AI voice agents?

They are a separate subscription from the seat. AI Receptionist starts at €99 a month for 200 minutes; AI Specialist is €349 a month with 1,000 minutes included; above 10,000 minutes a month CloudTalk quotes custom volume pricing it advertises as low as €0.15 a minute. The 14-day trial includes 50 AI voice agent minutes, which is enough to test the flow and not enough to test the load.

Are those prices in euros or dollars?

Both appear on the same page. Read from an EU address on 16 August 2026 the plans and add-ons quote in euros, while the AI bundle also shows $99 for what the euro block prices at €99. CloudTalk told us on 31 August 2026 that this is intentional: they keep the same figure in both currencies instead of converting at the exchange rate, so €99 and $99 are the same bundle. Treat the currency as part of the quote and confirm it in your own checkout rather than converting ours.

What does cancelling cost, and what happens to my numbers and recordings?

Read the cancellation summary before you confirm it. CloudTalk's help article How to change or cancel your subscription (read 6 October 2026) says the cancellation summary warns of permanent loss of call logs, recordings and statistics, loss of your numbers and users, no recovery afterwards, and non-refundable credit and subscription charges. On some plans you must book an offboarding call first, and the account stays active and billable until you finish the cancellation yourself. Numbers are released when the cancellation takes effect and generally cannot be recovered, so port them out before. An annual plan cannot be switched to monthly in the dashboard: the annual commitment has to be fulfilled first.

Can I cut seats mid-contract?

Yes, in the Billing page, but the lower charge starts with your next billing cycle, not on the day you change it (same article, read 6 October 2026). Fees billed on their own cycle, such as the monthly US A2P 10DLC messaging campaign fee, carry on at their usual rate. If an account manager makes the reduction for you, CloudTalk advises getting written confirmation before the next billing date.

What is not covered by the plan, so it draws on credit?

Credit is a prepaid wallet for anything outside your plan's calling package. CloudTalk's article What uses credit? (read 6 October 2026) lists calls to destinations outside the package, calls made from a number outside the package region, premium and special numbers, inbound calls to toll-free numbers, the forwarded leg of a call to an external phone, and every SMS, MMS and WhatsApp message, since there is no messaging bundle. Credit stays with the account it was bought on and, per the cancellation article, is not refunded when you cancel.

What should I weigh before buying Communication & Voice software?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.
