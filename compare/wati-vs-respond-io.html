Wati vs respond.io: one WhatsApp inbox, or every chat channel in one place?

Customers ask about price, delivery and order status on WhatsApp, and the chats pile up on one shared phone where staff step on each other. If you are comparing these two, you are probably a small team that has outgrown that phone and wants a shared inbox. The worry is the bill: seats, contacts and message fees each run on their own meter.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerWati fits a team that lives on WhatsApp and has three people or fewer: Growth is €59 a month billed yearly with 3 users (read 12 Sep 2026). respond.io fits a team that answers on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, SMS and live chat in one inbox, and it bills by monthly active contacts rather than seats. If automation is why you want respond.io, budget for Growth at $159 a month billed yearly, because Starter has no workflows or broadcasts (read 11 Oct 2026). The deciding factor is how many channels you answer on.

Which one fits you

Pick Wati if

  • WhatsApp is your only channel: our respond.io dossier points WhatsApp-only teams to Wati, since respond.io's entry plan is built around five channels.
  • You are three people or fewer: Wati Growth at €59 a month billed yearly (€69 monthly) includes 3 users.
  • You sell on Shopify: Wati's Shopify link is a $4.99 a month add-on, and buyers say teams answer price, delivery and order-status questions once through its inbox, chatbot and broadcasts.

When to pick neither

Your customers are mostly not on WhatsApp and your message volume is small. Wati's plan fee plus message costs is not worth it for a trickle, and respond.io's omnichannel plan is more than a single-channel inbox needs.

Look at Freshchat instead. Our Wati dossier names Freshchat for businesses whose customers are mostly not on WhatsApp. Read our Freshchat review.

What it costs at a real size

A team of six on yearly billing. Wati: Growth stops at 3 users, so you need Pro with 5 users plus one extra seat: €119 + 1 × €39 = €158 a month (read 12 Sep 2026), before Meta's per-conversation fees and metered add-ons. respond.io: Starter with 5 users plus one extra: $79 + 1 × $12 = $91 a month, but with no workflows or broadcasts; Growth includes 10 users at $159 a month (read 11 Oct 2026), before contacts above its starting 1,000. Wati served us euros and respond.io dollars, so we did not convert between them.

Questions buyers ask

Does a bigger Wati plan lower the per-message cost?

No. Meta's per-conversation fees are the same on every tier. Upgrading buys seats, automation triggers, AI credits and extra numbers, and those are metered too.

Who counts as a monthly active contact on respond.io?

A contact who sends or receives a message (broadcasts excluded), or makes or receives a call, in the billing month. Above your tier, extra contacts are billed per block of 100, and the rate is not printed (Billing & Usage article, read 11 Oct 2026).

Can I bring my old WhatsApp chats into Wati?

No. Wati's help article (read 25 Oct 2026) says importing existing chat history is not supported, apart from a one-time sync during WhatsApp Coexistence onboarding.

Why do I see Wati prices in a different currency?

We were served euros from 14 Aug 2026 on, while our 23 Jul 2026 dollar reading gave Growth $39, Pro $79 and Business $199 billed yearly. Take the figure from your own checkout.

What did our respond.io test show?

On 31 Oct 2026 the trial ran 7 days on Growth with no card. The CSV import would not continue without a phone number column, so we added the contacts by hand. We did not connect WhatsApp.
