Aircall vs Quo: the price gap is real, so is the reliability gap

Calls, texts and voicemails land on personal phones and nobody knows who answered a customer. Or your reps call from HubSpot or Salesforce all day and you want every call logged without extra steps. You probably run a small business or a sales or support team, and you are comparing Aircall and Quo to find out how much phone system you need and what it really costs.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerFor a team of three or more that lives inside a CRM, Aircall wins: buyers on G2, Trustpilot, TrustRadius and Reddit (Aug 2026) rate its HubSpot and Salesforce integration highest. It starts at $30 per licence a month billed annually, with a minimum of 3 licences (read 10 Oct 2026). Quo wins for a micro-team of two to five working mostly on Wi-Fi: Starter is $15 per user a month billed annually (read 11 Oct 2026), with one shared inbox for calls, texts and voicemail. The deciding factor is call reliability off Wi-Fi. G2 reviewers report dropped calls once Quo users leave Wi-Fi, so test it on mobile data before you commit. Aircall has call quality complaints too.

Which one fits you

Pick Aircall if

  • Your reps call from HubSpot or Salesforce all day. Buyers on G2, Trustpilot, TrustRadius and Reddit (Aug 2026) rate Aircall's CRM integration highest. Professional is $50 per licence a month billed annually, $70 monthly (read 10 Oct 2026).
  • You need routing, a power dialer and outbound campaigns. Professional adds smart routing, Power Dialer, outbound campaigns and AI call summaries on top of Essentials.
  • Your team grows past ten. Quo's Starter is capped at 10 paid users and its reviewers report reliability limits for larger teams; Aircall offers Enterprise with custom pricing from 25 users.

Pick Quo if

  • You are a team of two to five and want a business line cheaply. Quo Starter is $15 per user a month billed annually, $19 monthly (read 11 Oct 2026), with no seat minimum stated. Aircall's 3-licence minimum means its cheapest bill is $90 a month.
  • Your problem is calls and texts landing on personal phones. G2 and Capterra reviewers (Aug 2026) rate Quo 4.7 across 3,200+ reviews and say the shared inbox for calls, texts and voicemail keeps them organised.
  • You want an AI to answer some calls without a separate plan. Quo's Sona agent is on every plan with 1,000 credits a month, enough for ten calls; more credits start at $25 a month.

When to pick neither

You need a full contact-center suite with omnichannel routing. Aircall's own review says to skip it for that, and Quo is built for small teams, with no 24/7 support on lower tiers and shallow CRM integrations.

Look at a dedicated contact-center platform built for omnichannel routing. Neither dossier names one for this case.

What it costs at a real size

A team of five, billed annually. Aircall Essentials: 5 × $30 = $150 a month; Professional: 5 × $50 = $250 (read 10 Oct 2026). Quo Starter: 5 × $15 = $75 a month; Business: 5 × $23 = $115 (read 11 Oct 2026). Not in those sums: on Aircall, $6 a month per extra phone number and AI voice minutes beyond the 50 included on Essentials; on Quo, $5 a month per extra number, Campaign Registry fees of $19.50 once plus $1.50 to $3 a month, and taxes the page does not quantify. For a team of two, Aircall still bills 3 licences: 3 × $30 = $90 a month.

Questions buyers ask

Which is cheaper for a small team?

Quo, by a wide margin on the sticker. Five users on Quo Starter are 5 × $15 = $75 a month billed annually; five Aircall Essentials licences are 5 × $30 = $150 (read 10 and 11 Oct 2026). Both add charges for extra numbers and AI use, and Quo adds taxes it does not quantify on the page.

Which has more reliable calls?

Neither is free of complaints. On G2, Aircall reviewers mention dropped connections 71 times and poor call quality 58 times (Aug 2026). Quo's G2 reviewers report hundreds of connection problems and shaky calls off Wi-Fi. We have not tested either, so use the trial to call from mobile data where your team works.

Can I take my data with me if I leave Quo?

Partly. Quo's help article (read 11 Oct 2026) says Owners can export contacts, call logs and message logs as CSV, but not recordings, transcripts, AI summaries or voicemail files. Workspace data is deleted 30 days after cancelling. We did not compare Aircall's export.
