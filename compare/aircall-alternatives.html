Aircall alternatives: six phone systems and who they suit better

Aircall sells licences in threes, so a solo founder or a two-person team pays for seats nobody uses. The monthly rate is steep next to annual billing, and add-ons, extra numbers and AI minutes sit on top of the licence. Readers here are usually small businesses that need a business line, or teams weighing call quality against price.

Most tools on this page are affiliate partners of ours, Aircall included: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button here, at no extra cost to you. That is why the page also says when to stay with Aircall.

Short answerSolo founders and duos should look at UniTel Voice first, and micro-teams that work on Wi-Fi at Quo. Teams that live in a CRM and call abroad are closest to Aircall with CloudTalk, while CallHippo and KrispCall are budget options for US or multi-country calling. UK businesses calling UK customers can look at bOnline. Stay with Aircall if you have three or more reps calling from HubSpot or Salesforce all day.

Why people look beyond Aircall

  • Three-licence minimum: Aircall's comparison table sets a minimum of 3 users, so Essentials starts at $90 a month on annual billing and a two-person team pays for three seats (pricing page, 10 October 2026).
  • Steep monthly premium: Essentials is $30 per licence on annual billing against $40 monthly, and Professional $50 against $70.
  • Extras stack on the licence: each additional number is $6 a month (read 6 November 2026), AI Voice Agent minutes beyond the allowance cost $0.29 a minute (read 12 November 2026), and add-ons such as Analytics+ ($15) are per seat.
  • Call quality complaints: on G2, dropped connections come up 71 times and poor call quality 58 times (buyer reports, August 2026).

Aircall today: From $30 per licence/mo on annual billing ($40 monthly), minimum 3 licences (read 10 Oct 2026)

Questions buyers ask

What is the cheapest Aircall alternative for one person?

UniTel Voice Solo is $6.99 a month on yearly billing for one user and one number. Aircall's three-licence minimum puts its entry price at $90 a month on annual billing.

Which alternative keeps a strong CRM integration?

CloudTalk is the closest, a call centre built around CRM integrations. Quo's HubSpot app scores about 3.3 out of 5, and UniTel Voice has no CRM integrations according to buyer reports.

Is Aircall cheaper in euros?

No. On 10 October 2026 the Netherlands storefront showed EUR 30 and EUR 50 for Essentials and Professional, the same numerals as the US dollar prices rather than a converted amount.
