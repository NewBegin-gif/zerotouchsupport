            How the check works

            Support bills can change from month to month, so the check works with totals: seats, AI resolutions and phone costs from the same invoice together. A monthly total becomes a 12-month figure by multiplying it by twelve; a yearly total stays as it is and is also shown per month. This is for comparing costs, not a forecast of when money leaves your account.

            For another helpdesk, the check takes its 12-month figure from yours to get the difference before switching. Moving tickets, the knowledge base and phone numbers is counted as hours times an hourly value, plus implementation, the extra cost of running both tools during the move, and other one-off fees. A simple payback in months appears only when every one of those costs is known and the new tool costs less.

            The result picks one next step by fixed priority: first any amount that is missing or does not match, then a notice date already passed, then the comparison, then agent licences nobody used, then a notice deadline still unknown. It never ranks tools, and it does not call staying the best choice when you have not compared anything.

            What to check before support tools renew

            Agent licences

            Set paid agent licences against the agents who handled tickets or calls last month. Managers who only read reports may need a cheaper role or none. Ask about a minimum number of licences before you plan a reduction.

            AI resolutions and outcomes

            AI features can be billed per resolution or outcome on top of agent seats; then the seat price is not the whole bill. Take the AI charges from your last invoices, ask how a resolution is counted, and whether there is a monthly cap.

            Telephony

            Phone numbers, call minutes, recordings and regional numbers can be billed apart from the helpdesk. Check who holds the numbers, whether they can move to another provider, and what porting costs.

            Ticket and knowledge-base export

            Ticket history and help articles can take time to move. Ask which formats the export uses, whether attachments, tags and internal notes come along, and whether the new tool can import them without a paid service.

            Overlap during the move

            If support cannot stop during the migration, you may run both tools for a while. Enter only the extra cost of that period as overlap, not a full year of both.

            What this check does not do

            • It does not convert currencies. Enter both amounts in the same currency.
            • It does not give tax advice. Compare amounts that both include tax or both exclude it.
            • It does not multiply a list price by seats. Minimums, included seats, tiers and add-ons change the bill.
            • It does not work out a notice deadline from a notice period. Use the date your contract states, or ask your supplier.
            • It does not rank or recommend products. A list price appears only as a reference line while it is at most 30 days old.
            • The payback is a simple estimate, not a cash-flow forecast.

            Questions about the check

            Why not enter the price per agent?

            Because agent seats may not be the whole bill. AI resolutions, phone minutes and add-ons can change the total from month to month. Enter your invoice total, or an average of recent invoices, and the check leaves it as it is.

            How do I handle a bill that changes every month?

            Use the total of your last invoice or an average of the last three, and keep a note for yourself of which one you used. Ask the supplier for a quote for the next term that includes the AI and phone usage you expect.

            What should I count for moving tickets and articles?

            The hours to export and import tickets and the knowledge base, rebuild macros, routing and integrations, port phone numbers and train agents, plus any fees. Unknown costs stay unknown.

            Why can the first year show a loss?

            When switching costs are higher than one year of lower prices, the difference in the first 12 months is negative. The payback in months is a simple estimate of how long the lower price takes to cover the switching costs, not a cash-flow forecast.

            Does the calendar reminder hold any figures?

            No. The file holds one all-day event on a date you choose, titled Review software renewal unless you change it. Whether your calendar sends an alert depends on the app.
