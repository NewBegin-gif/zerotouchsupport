Typewise: AI that clears the repetitive tickets so your team handles the hard ones

Most support inboxes follow the 80/20 rule painfully closely: a large share of tickets are the same handful of questions, asked again and again. Having skilled people retype those answers all day is the fastest route to burnout — and it's also the most automatable work in the whole operation. This is the heart of what "zero-touch support" actually means.

When the heavy lifting is handled by autonomous systems, you earn the freedom to step away from the screen.

What I wanted wasn't a basic chatbot that deflects people into a dead end — it's a system that genuinely resolves the routine while keeping the quality sharp. That's where Typewise stands out, and it's the most direct fit for this whole site's premise.

What exactly is Typewise?

Typewise is an AI customer-service layer that sits on top of the tools you already run (Zendesk, Salesforce, your email inbox). Rather than offering spell-checks, it uses AI agents to classify cases, pull data from your CRM, and resolve customer requests end-to-end — with your team in the loop where it matters.

Why it's the clearest zero-touch fit

A real zero-touch setup doesn't mean dropping quality; it means letting automation do the repetitive lifting safely. Typewise is built around exactly that:

  • End-to-end resolution: using plain-language instructions (no code), you train it to handle whole workflows — a return, a quote, a billing update — on its own.
  • Human-in-the-loop: for sensitive or complex cases it drafts the response and flags it for a person to approve or tweak, so you stay in control while cutting handling time.
  • Native integration: it reads and writes across your existing systems, pulling order data and updating records without manual copy-paste.
  • Consistent brand voice: trained on your own knowledge base and vocabulary, so replies stay accurate and on-tone rather than generic.

The honest caveat: this is an enterprise-grade layer, so it earns its keep when you have real ticket volume to automate — not for a handful of emails a week. But once repetitive tickets are genuinely eating your team's days, Typewise turns support from a manual cost centre into something that mostly runs itself.
