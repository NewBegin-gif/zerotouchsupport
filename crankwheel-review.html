Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 21 Aug 2026

A free plan with no payment details required — unlimited usage for the first 15 days, then up to 15 sessions a month for life. Solo is $29/month for one user with unlimited usage, up to 30 viewers per share and up to 10 video-conferencing participants. Team starts at $99/month for unlimited users sharing 100 meetings a month, rising in steps: Team 200 $194, Team 500 $467, Team 1000 $882, Team 2000 $1,562.

Busy User discount plans cost less for a capped number of users — Team 100 at $59 for three users, Team 500 at $287 for fifteen. Enterprise is quoted per year and the page prints no figure. A meeting counts at 40 seconds with a viewer, or when you create a share link or segment, or on an Instant Demo call. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It's deliberately narrow — instant, no-download screen sharing for sales calls — so it's excellent at that one job and thin everywhere else: no full video-conferencing suite, no deep collaboration or webinar tooling. If you want an all-purpose meeting platform, this isn't it; if you want prospects to see your screen in seconds with zero friction, that focus is exactly why it works.

What CrankWheel does

No-download screen sharing for sales calls — a prospect clicks a link and sees your screen on any device. Deliberately narrow: no video conferencing, webinars or collaboration. The free plan covers one user and 15 meetings a month; Solo is about $29/user/month.

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run CrankWheel ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise

  • • Occasional resolution and scrolling issues. $29/month individual, $99 team.

When the paid plan is worth it

The ladder runs in a direction that catches people out. Solo is $29 a month for one user with unlimited usage; Team starts at $99 a month for unlimited users, but those users share a pool of 100 meetings a month. Upgrading therefore trades unlimited meetings for a shared allowance, so a single rep who runs a lot of calls is better served by the cheaper plan than by the bigger-sounding one.

Read the meter before deciding. A meeting is counted every time you host a viewer for 40 seconds or more, create a share link or a segment from a recording, or call a prospect in an Instant Demo session. Limits reset on your signup date. Volume buys less than you would expect — Team 100 is $99, Team 1000 is $882 and Team 2000 is $1,562, so the cost per meeting falls only from $0.99 to $0.78 across a twentyfold increase.

What upgrading does not solve is a small, busy team: for that shape the Busy User discount plans are cheaper, at $59 for three users sharing the same 100 meetings.

How to actually use CrankWheel

Start on the free plan rather than the trial page, because they are the same signup: no payment details are required, usage is unlimited for the first 15 days, and after that the account keeps up to 15 sessions a month for life. Launch a session from the browser or the extension and choose what to share — a browser tab, a single program window, or the whole screen — then send the link by email, text or instant message.

The viewer side is the point of the product: they connect in one click and are never asked to sign up or install anything, on any browser and any device, and the app shows you when they connect and how they engage. Two limits worth knowing before your first real call: granting a viewer remote control requires Windows or macOS on the presenter's side, and the meeting counter starts at 40 seconds, so short check-in calls still draw down a monthly allowance.
