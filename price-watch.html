Price Watch: what these vendors quietly changed

Researched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-02

Vendors raise prices quietly. We write it down.

Every month we archive the pricing page of every tool we cover, then compare the new capture against the last one. What follows is only the part that concerns the tools on this site, and only the changes a person confirmed by reading the raw lines of both months. Amounts on their own are not evidence: a currency selector or a failed capture can move a number without anything changing.

Caught by our own captures: 2026-07 → 2026-08

We compared 237 tools that had a capture in both months. 24 pages moved; a person read the raw lines and confirmed which were real. Below are the 4 that concern tools on this site, checked on 2026-08-22.

FEES UP

CallHippo

The plan prices held, but the two charges that sit outside them both rose: the one-time setup fee by a third, and the per-minute rate you pay past your bundle by more than half. Neither shows up in a tier comparison.

Caught by our own captures: 2026-08 → 2026-09

We compared 330 tools that had a capture in both months. 30 pages moved; a person read the raw lines and confirmed which were real. Below is the 1 that concerns a tool on this site, checked on 2026-09-02.

LADDER REBUILT, TOP UP 32%

Salesmessage

Our 2 August capture still showed the old five-step ladder from $25 to $249 a month. By our 8 August reading the page sold two plans: Basic at $25 and Pro at $329 a month, or $277 a month billed yearly, with everything in between replaced by a credits dropdown and published add-on rates of $10 per extra seat and $5 per extra number. The September capture carries the $277 figure. Read monthly against monthly, the top of the ladder went from $249 to $329, a 32% rise.

How we track this

Once a month we capture the vendor’s own pricing page and keep the file. The next month we capture it again and compare the two. A script proposes candidates; a person reads the raw lines of both months and decides what actually happened. Currency selectors, failed captures and feature changes that move a number without moving a price are thrown out at that step.
