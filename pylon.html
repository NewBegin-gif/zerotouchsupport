How to deliver VIP support to B2B clients via Slack (without the chaos in your own inbox)

By the ZeroTouchSupport Team | Reading time: 6 minutes

If you serve B2B clients, you already know that traditional email support tickets are slowly dying out. Your best, highest-paying clients want to communicate directly. They want to create a shared Slack or Microsoft Teams channel with you. While this is fantastic for building strong relationships, it is an absolute nightmare for your support operations. Questions get buried, tasks are forgotten, and performance metrics become impossible to track.

The solution to this modern operational bottleneck is called Pylon. It is literally built to bridge the gap between the frictionless chat experience your client wants, and the structured backend system your team desperately needs.

Order in the Chat Chaos

When a client asks a question in your shared Slack channel, your team doesn't have to dive directly into that messy chat thread. Pylon intercepts the message, turns it into an organized ticket in the background, and routes it to the central dashboard where your team actually works. As soon as your agent replies, Pylon seamlessly posts that reply back into the client's Slack channel as if they were right there.
